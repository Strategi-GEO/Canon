# Repurpose

**Repurpose** is a nav row with nothing behind it yet. Open it and you get one card that reads "Repurpose is coming soon", with a sentence saying it will turn shipped blogs into social posts, emails and briefs, so one researched piece feeds every channel.

There is no button on the page, on purpose. A control that leads nowhere teaches you not to trust the ones that work.

!!! tip "You are not missing a step"
    Nothing in Canon depends on this tab today. No blog is waiting there, and no work is stuck behind it. You can walk past it.

## How it differs from the channel tabs

The word "repurpose" describes what the four channel tabs already do, which is where the confusion comes from. The difference is scope.

**LinkedIn**, **Medium**, **Bluesky** and **X** each own one platform end to end: pick blogs, generate, review, send to the client, post it, mark it posted. That work is live now and it is documented in [LinkedIn, Medium, Bluesky and X](channels.md).

**Repurpose** is the planned home for the formats that have no tab of their own, the emails and the briefs named on that card. Until it ships, the four channel tabs are the whole of what Canon can cut from a finished blog.

If you came here looking for the LinkedIn post or the X thread, go to the tab with that name.
