## §6 Do-not-claim list (BINDING)

### §6.1 Absolutely prohibited (body, FAQ, tables, headings, alt text, anywhere)
1. **Investment returns of any kind.** No appreciation percentages, no rental-yield figures, no ROI,
   no payback maths, no "guaranteed", "assured", or "expected" returns. This includes the ROI framing
   used in Vacation Village's own brochure and the "capital appreciation potential" language in the
   internal about file.
2. **Market-growth statistics taken from Vacation Village's or Agrocorp's own press releases.** The
   "18 to 22% annual growth" figure in the April 2026 release is Vacation Village's own claim.
   Source every market-growth claim from an independent, India-specific third party instead.
3. **Legal, title, or approval status beyond RERA.** No "clear title", no "clean title", no DTCP,
   no land-conversion or NA-land status, no khata, no encumbrance claims. The RERA number is the only
   confirmed approval. "Development sanctions received" is permitted **only** as a Vacation Village
   announcement (§5), never as a description of a specific approval a buyer can rely on.
4. **Possession, handover, or completion dates.** None are confirmed anywhere.
5. **Maintenance, CAM, or club fees.** No figures exist in any verified source.
6. **Specific loan-to-value or financing percentages.** The CKM kit's "bank will finance 70%" was
   dropped from the live-site FAQ; only "the project is approved from all leading banks" is safe.
7. **Unsubstantiated superlatives:** best, first, only, number one, largest, most. Vacation Village's
   own blog calls the project "the first gated second-home community in India's coffee belt built to
   a mandatory Neo-Classical code"; that is unverified marketing copy and must not be repeated.
8. **The Manor as part of the project's amenities.** See §4.
9. **Agrocorp "13 million sq. ft. delivered."** Internal about file only, contradicted in scale by the
   live site's own counters. Do not publish. See §7.
10. **Agrocorp "1,200+ acres delivered / transacted."** Conflicted: the live-site body counter reads
    1,200+ *satisfied customers* and 350+ acres under management. Do not publish. See §7.
11. **The ₹48 lakh to ₹68 lakh price band** from the internal about file. Superseded. See §7.
12. **Map coordinates.** Two conflicting sets appear on the live site. Do not publish coordinates.
13. **Any claim about Sakleshpur, Madikeri, Coorg, or any competing location that is not carried by a
    fetched, independent, India-specific source.** Comparison content must not editorialise a rival
    town into a weakness that no source supports.
