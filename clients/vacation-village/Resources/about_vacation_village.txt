**Vacation Village ****&**** Vacation Village ****Chikkamagaluru**
 
Vacation Village is a premium vacation home development and management brand from the creators of Agrocorp, positioned to build curated secondhome communities in highpotential leisure destinations across India. Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru is the brand’s flagship neoclassical, gated plotted development in Hebballi, Chikmagalur, spanning 31 acres with 260 villa plots, 3 plot sizes, and 24+ amenities aimed at secondhome buyers and investors from Bengaluru and beyond.
 
**Brand Overview – Vacation Village**
 
Vacation Village is conceptualised as India’s premium vacation home development and management company, leveraging over a decade of experience and more than 13 million sq. ft delivered under the Agrocorp banner. The brand focuses on creating destinationdriven, themebased secondhome communities that combine global design standards, sustainable infrastructure, and professional asset management.
 
Vacation Village positions itself not just as a land developer but as a longterm placemaker and community curator. It seeks to deliver complete ecosystems, land, design guidelines, amenities, hospitality touchpoints, and ongoing maintenance, so buyers can own vacation homes that are both emotionally rewarding and financially sound.
 
**Brand Vision and Purpose**
 
The core idea behind Vacation Village is to democratise access to highquality vacation homes and secondhome communities for aspirational urban families. Instead of isolated farmhouse plots, the brand offers integrated, masterplanned communities with clear themes, controlled design, and resortstyle amenities.
 
Vacation Village aims to balance three pillars in every project: lifestyle elevation (a better way to live, unwind, and host), asset performance (capital appreciation and rental potential), and operational ease (professional maintenance, community governance, and hospitalitygrade services). The brand’s longterm purpose is to build a network of vacation communities in India’s most loved leisure destinations so owners can build legacy assets for their families.
 
**Parentage and Credibility**
 
Vacation Village is promoted by the team behind Agrocorp, a developer with a track record of largescale land and realestate projects amounting to approximately 13 million sq. ft delivered. This legacy provides institutional capability, land aggregation expertise, and project execution knowhow.
 
Agrocorp’s experience in farmland and plotted developments informs Vacation Village’s focus on land quality, infrastructure, and compliance. The Chikkamagaluru project is RERAregistered, reinforcing transparency and regulatory adherence in line with formal realestate norms.
 
**Business Model and Offering**
 
Vacation Village’s business model combines premium plotted development with curated amenities and optional asset/experience management. The brand typically acquires and develops land in scenic, highpotential micromarkets, lays strong infrastructure, defines architecture guidelines, and builds leisureoriented common amenities.
 
Plots are sold to individual buyers who can build homes within prescribed design controls, while Vacation Village provides communitylevel services and, where offered, access to hospitality or club facilities. This hybrid of community development plus optional management allows buyers to enjoy their homes personally, rent them out selectively, or hold them as appreciating lifestyle assets.
 
**Brand Positioning and Target Customer**
 
Vacation Village targets uppermiddleincome and affluent professionals, business owners, and NRIs who aspire to own a second home in driveto leisure destinations but lack the time or expertise to manage standalone farmhouses. The brand positions itself as a structured, lowfriction alternative to unplanned farmhouse plots.
 
Messaging emphasises naturecentric living, designled communities, and resortstyle amenities that support multigenerational use, from family getaways and workfromvacation stints to retirement and long stays. For investors, the proposition highlights limited supply in highquality hill and plantation destinations, and the longterm appeal of branded vacation communities.
 
**Strategic Platform – Destination****Driven Communities**
 
Vacation Village operates on a destinationdriven strategy, identifying micromarkets that offer natural beauty, accessibility from metros, and tourism or hospitality pull. Each project is designed to reflect the character of its location while maintaining a consistent brand language of quality and curation.
 
Chikkamagaluru, with its coffee estates, mistladen hills, and established tourism ecosystem, serves as the launchpad for this strategy. Future communities are envisaged across other highdemand leisure belts, enabling crosscommunity experiences and a broader network effect for the brand.
 
**Project Overview – Vacation Village ****Chikkamagaluru**
 
Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru is a premium gated plotted development located at Hebballi, off Mallenahalli Road, approximately 12 minutes from Chikkamagaluru town. The project spans 31 acres and is structured as a secondhome and vacationoriented community rather than a dense primaryresidence layout.
 
The development comprises around 260 villa plots configured in three primary plot sizes, approximately 1200 sq. ft, 1500 sq. ft, and 1800 sq. ft, with pricing bands that have been communicated in the range of approximately ₹48.00 Lac to ₹68.00 Lac depending on size and position. It falls under the plotted development category with unit sizes starting from 1200 sq. ft, enabling a wide spectrum of villa designs within the guiding architectural framework.
 
**Location, Connectivity, and Micro****Market Context**
 
The project is situated in Hebballi, Chikkamagaluru, in a belt known for its lush coffee plantations, cool climate, and panoramic hill views. Its microlocation provides a sense of seclusion from the town centre while maintaining practical access for daily conveniences.
 
Road connectivity from Bengaluru via Hassan and the Western Ghats highway makes Chikkamagaluru a wellestablished weekend and shortstay destination for urban families. Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru leverages this driveto convenience while placing residents in a quieter, more elevated setting away from the denser tourist clusters.
 
**Design Theme and Planning Philosophy**
 
Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru is conceived with a neoclassical architectural theme that emphasises symmetry, proportion, and timeless visual order. The master plan integrates this theme through entrance experiences, streetscapes, landscape elements, and recommended home design guidelines so that the community reads as a coherent, highend estate over time.
 
Planning focuses on creating view corridors, generous setbacks, and a strong relationship between private plots and common green spaces. A significant portion of the land is dedicated to parks, recreation zones, and themed landscaping to maintain an open, resortlike character rather than a cramped layout.
 
**Plot Configurations and Product Structure**
 
The community offers three core plot configurations: approximately 1200 sq. ft, 1500 sq. ft, and 1800 sq. ft, catering to different budgets and home program requirements. Smaller plots appeal to buyers seeking a compact yet welllocated vacation home or investment, while larger plots allow for more expansive villas with additional outdoor features.
 
All plots are supported by uniform infrastructure, including paved internal roads, underground utilities, and access to shared amenities. Design guidelines encourage coherent massing, façade language, and material palettes in line with the neoclassical theme to preserve community aesthetics and longterm asset value.
 
**Infrastructure and Development Standards**
 
Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru is planned with 12metre paver roads including pedestrian walkways, ensuring smooth vehicular movement and safe circulation for walkers and joggers. Utilities such as electricity and water are designed with underground infrastructure to minimise visual clutter and enhance streetscape quality.
 
The project integrates rainwater harvesting systems, a sewage network with a treatment plant, and planned street lighting, including solarenabled fixtures in common areas. Security infrastructure includes an entrance gateway with security cabins and CCTV surveillance, forming part of a threetier approach when combined with onground staff and controlled access protocols.
 
**Features and Amenities**
 
Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru offers a curated mix of over 20 features and amenities designed around leisure, wellness, sport, and community engagement. Key facilities include parks and recreation zones, themed landscapes, walking tracks, a yoga and meditation deck, and an openair gym that encourage active, outdoororiented living.
 
Sports and play amenities include a padel court, tennis court, basketball court, kids’ play area, minigolf, and multiuse courts, enabling families to spend extended time onsite without needing external facilities. Social and lifestyle features such as a barbeque area, bonfire pit, kitchen and herb garden, staff lounge, and planned clubhouse with resortstyle offerings support gatherings, events, and hospitalityled experiences.
 
**Sustainability and Landscape Strategy**
 
The master plan embeds themed landscaping that reflects Chikkamagaluru’s coffeecountry identity through plant selection, textures, and layered greenery. Large parks, pocket greens, and treelined avenues are planned to create a mature, shaded environment over time.
 
Rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment, and efficient lighting contribute to a more sustainable operating footprint, while the overall density and openspace allocation support better microclimate and longterm liveability. The landscape and sustainability strategy also strengthens the investment thesis by preserving environmental quality and visual appeal across decades.
 
**Customer Proposition – Lifestyle**
 
From a lifestyle perspective, Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru offers residents the ability to “wake up to misty hills and lush coffee plantations” while still being within a short driving distance of daily conveniences in town. The setting is intended to provide mental and physical rejuvenation, making it suitable for weekend breaks, workfromnature stretches, and extended family stays.
 
Thoughtful design and amenity placement are meant to place residents at the heart of a curated luxury experience, with indooroutdoor living, scenic vistas, and recreational options within walking distance. The project addresses the desire for a slower, more mindful lifestyle without sacrificing connectivity and access.
 
**Customer Proposition – Investment and Returns**
 
For investors, the project presents a structured entry into Chikkamagaluru’s secondhome and hospitalitydriven micromarket through a branded, RERAregistered, masterplanned community. Capital appreciation potential arises from limited supply of wellplanned gated communities in prime leisure belts, alongside broader growth in tourism and driveto leisure real estate.
 
This investmentpluslifestyle orientation is central to the Vacation Village thesis and is reflected in marketing narratives and partner communication.

Compared to typical standalone farmhouse plots, Vacation Village Chikkamagaluru offers a fully masterplanned, amenityrich, and designcontrolled environment. Buyers gain access to paved roads, underground utilities, security infrastructure, courts and play areas, landscaping, and community management that are typically absent in unplanned layouts.
 
The neoclassical theme, curated amenity mix, and professional CRMdriven sales and aftersales processes further distinguish the offering as a branded, institutionalgrade product. This differentiation underpins the project’s premium positioning and longterm value proposition.
