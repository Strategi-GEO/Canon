Vacation Village is an Indian second-home developer that builds and manages villa-plot communities. Its site describes a themed-architecture approach where the location is selected first and the community designed around it, with a dedicated team handling grounds, security, and day-to-day running. Its latest launch is in Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka: 31 acres, 260 villa plots, a neo-classical architecture theme, and 24+ amenities, carrying RERA registration PRM/KA/RERA/1255/477/PR/280126/008430. The project is approved by Axis Bank and LIC Housing Finance, and a financing option lets buyers borrow against mutual funds, equities, and ESOPs. Owners also get access to The Manor, a separately owned resort lounge that the site states is not part of the project amenities. The enquiry form lists holiday home and investment as buyer interests.
