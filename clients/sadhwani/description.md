Sadhwani Real Estate Holdings is a Bengaluru-based real estate advisory firm founded in 1991 by Ramesh Sadhwani, operating from Lavelle Road in the city's Central Business District. The firm works in Bangalore's luxury and ultra-luxury property segment, offering four services: luxury residences (penthouses and villa estates), commercial leasing (typically 2,000 to 10,000 sq. ft.), investment advisory, and land acquisition. It is RERA-registered and a member of CREA and NAR. According to the site, it advises HNI investors, NRIs, family offices, institutional funds, multinational corporations, and residential buyers and sellers. Its stated approach is to present data and let clients decide.
