BLR Brewing Co. is a brewpub business operating dine-in locations in Bengaluru, India. The site lists outlets at Bellandur (Eco World), HSR Layout, Marathahalli (Prestige Tech Park), Kanakapura Road, Whitefield, and Electronic City, with a Yelahanka location marked as opening soon. It describes food and drink curated by a head chef and mixologist, a globally inspired menu, and spaces designed around the five elements, incorporating greenery, water elements, and vintage detailing. The site mentions live gigs, DJ nights, and Sunday brunch, and offers table reservations by outlet. Opening hours are 12:00pm to 11:30pm Monday and Tuesday, and 12:00pm to 1:00am Wednesday through Sunday.
