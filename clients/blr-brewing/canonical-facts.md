# canonical-facts.md: BLR Brewing (BINDING)

This file is the binding fact base for every blog this factory writes for BLR Brewing. Writers
treat §1 to §5 as true without rechecking, and the evaluator audits drafts against it. Nothing
outside this file is established: if a claim is not here, fetch a source or do not make it.

**The live domain wins.** On any conflict between https://www.blrbrewing.com/ and an uploaded
resource, the live site wins, and the conflict is recorded in §7 with its resolution.

**Read §8 before trusting §1 and §2.** The live site's outlet pages appear to have been written
from the same uploaded knowledge base this file draws on, so site agreement with the knowledge
base is frequently NOT independent corroboration. §7 #1 explains. This is the single most
important caveat in the document.

## §4 Adjacent and separate entities (never conflated)

**Beanlore** (also **Beanlore Coffee Roasters**) is a coffee brand operated by the same legal
entity, BLR Restaurants Private Limited, and it is NOT BLR Brewing Co. Every BLR Brewing page
footer carries: "Beanlore is a brand operated by BLR Restaurants Private Limited", linking to
http://www.beanlore.com/ ([Whitefield page](https://www.blrbrewing.com/whitefield)). Brewer World
describes Beanlore Coffee Roasters as an in-house cafe and "a brainchild of BLR Brewing Co.'s
co-founder Prasanna Kumar" ([30 Jan 2024](https://www.brewer-world.com/blr-brewing-co-introduces-indias-largest-waterfall-at-whitefield-brewpub-a-blend-of-nature-and-culinary-excellence/)).
The [group-packages](https://www.blrbrewing.com/group-packages) outlet dropdown lists Beanlore
HSR Layout, Beanlore Indiranagar and Beanlore Jayanagar as separate venues.

Rules: **Beanlore outlets are never counted among BLR Brewing's six outlets.** Beanlore is never
called a brewpub. A Beanlore location is never given as a BLR Brewing address. Where a piece
needs to mention the coffee programme, say that Beanlore is a separate brand operated by BLR
Restaurants Private Limited, and cite the footer.

**Hamilton** is a venue connected to the Whitefield outlet and operated by BLR Brewing Co.
(§2.5). It is not a seventh outlet and is never counted among the six. Its 300 to 400 covers are
stated separately from Whitefield's ~1,200 and must never be silently added into a chain total.

**Kriya** is an unrelated third-party SaaS whose metadata contaminates the group-packages page
(§3.1). It has no relationship to BLR Brewing's brand story. Never describe it as connected.

**Whitefield opening, announced 30 January 2024.** BLR Brewing Co. announced the opening of its
fourth and largest brewpub, in Whitefield, spread across 65,000 sq ft with a seating capacity of
1,200 covers ([Brewer World](https://www.brewer-world.com/blr-brewing-co-introduces-indias-largest-waterfall-at-whitefield-brewpub-a-blend-of-nature-and-culinary-excellence/),

The same announcement states the brand had three outlets before Whitefield.

**The caveat that governs both articles.** They are near-identical trade-press reproductions of a
BLR Brewing press release: same phrasing, same figures, same founder quote. **Two outlets of the
same press release are one source, not two.** They are usable for the physical facts they carry
(square footage, cover count, opening sequence, address). They are NOT independent support for
any superlative or evaluative claim in them, including "India's largest waterfall", "pioneer in
traditional brew gardens", "award-winning craft beers" and "3 award winning outlets". Those are
the client's own marketing, printed verbatim, and §6.1 forbids them.

## §6 Do-not-claim list (BINDING)

### §6.1 Absolutely prohibited (body, FAQ, tables, headings, alt text, anywhere)

**House rules, always:**
1. **Unsubstantiated superlatives**: best, first, only, number one, leading, largest, biggest,
   unmatched, pioneer. This binds even when quoting the client's own PR (§5).
2. **Any statistic, price, date or specification not in this file or in a source fetched in full.**
3. **Any claim about a competitor not sourced to that competitor's own material.** Byg Brewski,
   Toit, Arbor, Geist, Windmills, Brewklyn, The Permit Room, Iron Hill, Social, Longboard and
   Tropica are named in the knowledge base as a competitive set. That is the founders' opinion,
   not a fact about those businesses. Never state a competitor's size, seat count, quality or
   footfall without that competitor's own source.
4. **Framing the client's projections, targets or marketing claims as independent fact.**

**Specific to BLR Brewing:**

5. **"India's largest waterfall", or any superlative about the waterfall.** The client's PR claims
   it; §7 #8 shows the same PR contradicts the site on the waterfall's height. Forbidden outright.
6. **Any waterfall height.** The site says 50-foot, the PR says 45-foot (§7 #8). Unresolved, so
   no number.
7. **Awards, award counts, and "award-winning".** No award is verified by a primary source (§9).
   Do not write "award-winning", do not name a category, do not count. This binds even though the
   client's own homepage meta description says "Award-winning". See §6.2 for what is permitted.
8. **Chain-wide opening hours as a single fact**, and any claim that hours are consistent across
   outlets. Google Business Profile contradicts it (§7 #4).
9. **Opening dates, timelines or availability for Yelahanka**, or any wording implying it is open.
   "Opening soon" is the only status the site supports, and "soon" is not a date.
10. **Any capacity, headcount or booking guarantee**: that a group of N will be accommodated, that
    a buyout is available, that a table will be held, that an outlet can seat a given party. Seat
    counts in §2.2 describe the venue, never a promise to a reader.
11. **Alcohol claims that a regulator would test.** No health, wellness or safety benefit of beer
    or of any drink. No claim about alcohol content, ABV, IBU or calories: none is sourced. No
    marketing that targets or appeals to under-25s, and never state or imply a legal drinking age
    without a source. Karnataka excise licensing, permits and registration numbers are not in this
    file: never assert the brand holds any licence or approval.
12. **Food safety, allergen, dietary and hygiene claims as guarantees.** The knowledge base
    describes allergen tagging, vegan options and Jain customisation. None is on the live site and
    none is verified. Never tell a reader a dish is gluten-free, nut-free, vegan, Jain or safe for
    an allergy. This is the rule most likely to cause real harm if broken.
13. **FSSAI numbers, licences, certifications or registrations.** Not verified, so never asserted.
14. **Prices, package pricing, minimum guest counts, buffet rates or cover charges.** The only
    price found is "Average meal for two (with Alcohol): INR 1500" in a January 2024 press
    release, which is stale and was never a rate card. Do not publish it or any other figure.
15. **Named staff other than Umang Nair.** Rajendra Nayak, Rajaram Gupta, the junior chefs, and
    the creative head named in the knowledge base appear in no public source, and the knowledge
    base itself requires verification before external use of named team members. Prasanna Kumar is
    permitted only as co-founder, per §6.2.
16. **The Siebel Institute claim in the knowledge base's stronger form.** Never write that only
    two or three brewers in India trained at Siebel: it is an unsourced claim about other people's
    credentials. See §6.2 for the permitted form.
17. **"Bangalore Brewing Co." as the brand's primary name.** No public source uses it (§7 #9).
18. **Beanlore counted as a BLR Brewing outlet**, or Hamilton counted as a seventh outlet (§4).
19. **"Seamless", "seamlessly", and the rest of the house banned list.** These appear in BLR
    Brewing's own site copy ("a seamless dining experience", "ensures a seamless experience") and
    in its PR ("seamlessly blending"). Never lift them. Paraphrase.
20. **Any operational figure in §9.** Guest counts, staff numbers, brew volume, repeat-customer
    share, the food-to-alcohol split, plant counts, koi valuations, party volumes. They are the
    brand's own unverified assertions until a human moves them up.

## §7 Known conflicts and their resolution

1. **The live site is not independent of the uploaded knowledge base. UNRESOLVED, and it
   undermines the normal precedence rule.** The `/whitefield` and `/blank-*` outlet pages repeat
   the knowledge base's figures and phrasing closely enough to be downstream of it: ~1,200 seats
   plus 300 to 400 at Hamilton, 15,000+ plants, a 50-foot waterfall, "more than 3,500 guests on a
   weekend day", "Siebel Institute-trained brewmaster Umang Nair", the Liquid Journey framing, the
   founder's-favourite dish. The knowledge base is dated May 2026 and was prepared by Strategi,
   the same agency running this factory, and the site also hosts this factory's `/articles/`
   output. **Resolution: where the site and the knowledge base agree on a brand-internal number,
   that is ONE source, not two.** Those figures are recorded in §9 or attributed as the brand's
   own statements in §2.2, never as corroborated fact. The precedence rule in the engine contract
   assumes the site is independent of the resources. Here it is not. A human should confirm who
   wrote the outlet pages and when.

2. **Phone numbers: the homepage contradicts itself. RESOLVED.** The homepage location cards give
   Kanakapura Road +91 80 4716 2222 and Electronic City +91 80 4710 8277. The FIND US block on the
   same page reverses them. Google Business Profile matches the cards: Electronic City
   +918047108277, Kanakapura Road +918047162222. **Resolution: the cards and GBP win. The FIND US
   block is wrong.** §2.1 carries the correct set. This is a live error sending callers to the
   wrong outlet and belongs in the operator's fix list (§8).

3. **Outlet count: six, seven, or eight. RESOLVED for publication.** The homepage says "six
   locations" and shows seven cards, the seventh being Yelahanka marked OPENING SOON. The
   group-packages page says "eight of our iconic spaces" and its dropdown lists twelve entries,
   which include three Beanlore cafes, Hamilton, a Corporate Office and The Galleria Mall.
   **Resolution: six operating BLR Brewing outlets.** The other entries are Beanlore (§4), a
   non-outlet venue (Hamilton), an office, and an unopened site. "Eight" counts things that are
   not brewpub outlets. Never repeat "eight".

4. **Opening hours: the site contradicts Google. UNRESOLVED.** The homepage asserts one schedule
   for all outlets (Mon to Tue to 11:30pm, Wed to Sun to 1:00am) and the knowledge base asserts
   hours "are consistent across all six locations", with Sunday closing at 12:30am. Google
   Business Profile shows neither: Electronic City records a 00:30 close every day including
   Monday and Tuesday; Kanakapura Road records 00:30 on Wednesday and Thursday but 1:00am on
   Friday; several outlets record a 00:30 Sunday close against the site's 1:00am. **Resolution:
   none available from fetched sources, and both are client-controlled.** §6.1 #8 forbids a
   chain-wide hours claim. Per-outlet hours cite that outlet's GBP and carry a date.

5. **Yelahanka's map link points at Electronic City. RESOLVED as a site bug.** The Yelahanka card
   and the Electronic City card both link `maps.app.goo.gl/zYCwKb7gL9FwcqXJ7`. **Resolution: the
   Yelahanka location is unestablished.** Never state where it is beyond the site's own words,
   "next to The Galleria Mall". Operator fix list (§8).

6. **Kanakapura Road's address. UNRESOLVED.** The site gives Paramount Gardens, Jyotipuram,
   560062. Google gives Vajarahalli, Paramount Gardens, Jyotipuram, 560109. Same complex, two
   pincodes. **Resolution: use the site's address, per precedence, and never publish the pincode
   alone.** Worth a human check; a wrong pincode misroutes deliveries and map pins.

7. **The founder's name is spelled "Prassana Kumar" in the Brewer World article** and "Prasanna
   Kumar" in the knowledge base and the ET headline coverage. **Resolution: "Prasanna Kumar".**
   The single-s spelling is a typo in one trade article.

8. **The waterfall is 45 feet or 50 feet. UNRESOLVED.** The live site and the knowledge base say
   50-foot. The January 2024 press release says "the mesmerizing 45-foot waterfall" in its body
   while its own headline calls it India's largest. **Resolution: publish no height** (§6.1 #6).
   The client's PR contradicting the client's site on a physical measurement is exactly the kind
   of claim a competitor would check.

9. **The brand's primary name. RESOLVED, and it contradicts the knowledge base's plan.** The
   knowledge base (§10.3, §10.4, §10.8) instructs that the primary entity is "Bangalore Brewing
   Co." with "BLR Brewing" as a consumer alias, and builds its canonical brand sentence and its
   whole schema plan on that. **No public source uses "Bangalore Brewing Co."** The homepage, every
   outlet page, the footer, both trade articles and all seven Google listings use "BLR Brewing Co."
